Welcome to Blackfoot High School!

"The Higher You Climb,

The Better The View!"

We Believe: BHS Faculty and Staff believe our students are our community’s greatest resource. Every student has a valued set of abilities and skills that require development to reach their full potential.

Our Mission: Partnering with parents and community leaders, our mission is to identify in each student areas of improvement to foster deeper learning.

Our Goals: To meet our students’ needs, BHS Faculty and Staff will develop greater leadership skills and use student-learning data to identify successes.
